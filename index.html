Methodology

The visualizer models annual returns as a normal distribution using the entered mean return and standard deviation.

X ~ N(μ, σ²)

The probability density function is:

f(x) = 1 / (σ√2π) × e−½((x−μ)/σ)²

Under the normal-distribution assumption, approximately 68.27% of observations fall within ±1 SD and approximately 95.45% within ±2 SD.

Target probabilities are calculated using the cumulative normal distribution between the specified minimum and maximum return.

Actual investment returns may differ substantially from a normal distribution. The calculated probabilities should therefore be treated as a theoretical statistical model, not as a forecast or guarantee.
